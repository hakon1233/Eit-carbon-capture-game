Key Data Verified (January 2026)

From Global Carbon Budget 2025

  • Fossil CO2 emissions 2025: 38.1 Gt/year (1.1% growth)
  • Land-use change emissions 2025: 4.1 Gt/year
  • Total CO2 emissions: ~42.2 Gt/year
  • Ocean CO2 sink: 29% of total emissions (~12.2 Gt/yr)
  • Land CO2 sink: 21% of total emissions (~8.9 Gt/yr)
  • Remaining carbon budget for 1.5°C: ~4 years at current emissions
  • Remaining carbon budget for 1.7°C: 525 GtCO2 (12 years)
  • Remaining carbon budget for 2.0°C: 1055 GtCO2 (25 years)

From IRENA 2024 Report

  • Solar PV LCOE: $0.043/kWh ($43/MWh global average)
  • Onshore Wind LCOE: $0.034/kWh ($34/MWh - cheapest source)
  • Offshore Wind LCOE: $0.078-0.080/kWh ($78-80/MWh)
  • 91% of new renewable capacity cheaper than fossil alternatives

From IPCC AR6

  • Tipping point risks emerge above 1°C
  • Risks become high around 2°C
  • Risks reach very high around 2.5-4°C
  • Current warming: ~1.2°C above pre-industrial

From World Bank GEP January 2025

  • Global growth: 2.7% (2025-2026)
  • Advanced economies: ~1.5%
  • Emerging markets (EMDEs): ~4% steady
  • Low-income countries: 5-5.7%
  • MENA (oil economies): 3.4-4.1%

From IEA Energy Efficiency 2024

  • Global average efficiency improvement: 1.0-1.8%/yr
  • Industry sector improvement: <0.5%/yr (lowest)
  • Developing economies (China, India): 2.5-4%/yr (catch-up effect)

From IPCC/NREL Lifecycle Assessments

  • Nuclear lifecycle emissions: 4-12 gCO2/kWh
  • Wind lifecycle emissions: ~4 gCO2/kWh (lowest)
  • Solar PV lifecycle emissions: 6-43 gCO2/kWh
  • Coal for comparison: 820-1200 gCO2/kWh

From Nordhaus DICE Model / IMF

  • GDP damage at 3°C warming: 2.1% (DICE model)
  • GDP damage at 6°C warming: 8.5% (DICE model)
  • IMF cumulative damage by 2100: 7.22% without mitigation
  • Traditional estimates: 1-3% per °C

From IPCC AR6 WG2 (Extreme Weather)

  • Precipitation extremes: +7% per 1°C (Clausius-Clapeyron)
  • 10-year extreme events: ~2x more frequent at 1.5°C
  • 10-year extreme events: ~2.7x more frequent at 2°C
  • Tropical cyclone intensity: +5% per °C

From ND-GAIN Country Index

  • Ranks 185 countries on climate vulnerability
  • Components: Exposure, Sensitivity, Adaptive Capacity
  • Highest vulnerability: Africa, South Asia
  • Lowest vulnerability: Northern Europe
