Climate Strategy Simulator

Carbon Capture: Climate Overseer

Steer the planet away from warming by investing in carbon solutions.

Goal

Reduce global temperature to +1.0°C by 2050, then hold it for 12 months in a row. Avoid tipping points that trigger irreversible climate effects.

Rules

CO2 rises monthly. Build projects to reduce emissions. Research technologies. Weather random events.

How to Play

Select regions, fund projects, run campaigns, and unlock achievements as you save the climate.
