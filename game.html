Climate Tipping Points

Tipping Points

Irreversible climate thresholds that permanently accelerate warming once crossed. Avoid these at all costs!

  • Arctic Ice (1.4°C) - Reduced reflectivity speeds warming
  • Permafrost (1.7°C) - Releases trapped methane
  • Amazon (2.1°C) - Forest dieback reduces CO2 absorption
  • Ocean Circulation (2.4°C) - Disrupts global weather

Tip: Watch for yellow "Warning" status when approaching a threshold.

Active Events

Random Events

Climate, political, and economic events that affect your game. Events can be positive or negative and last several months.

  • ▲ Green border - Positive effects
  • ▼ Red border - Negative effects
  • ● Yellow border - Mixed/neutral

Tip: Watch for rare Breakthrough events - they provide permanent bonuses!

🌪️ Active Disasters

Natural Disasters

Climate-related disasters that reduce regional income. Higher temperatures increase disaster frequency and severity.

  • ▼ marker - All disasters are negative (reduce income)
  • Income reduction - Disasters reduce regional climate finance
  • Climate awareness - Affected regions become easier to campaign in
  • Duration - Disasters last 1-6 months

Tip: Watch temperature - as it rises, disasters become more frequent!

Climate History

Climate History

Track your progress over time with three key metrics:

Tip: A downward CO2 trend means you're winning! Watch for inflection points.
