Game Rules & Mechanics

Click any section below to expand. Complete guide to playing Carbon Capture: Climate Overseer.

1 Objective & Win/Lose Conditions

Victory Condition

Reduce global temperature to ≤1.0°C above pre-industrial levels at any point during the game.

Temperature must drop to 1.0°C or below → You Win!

Defeat Conditions

  • Climate Catastrophe: Global temperature reaches ≥3.0°C
  • Time Limit: Year reaches 2100 without achieving victory

4 Core Mechanics & Formulas

Time System

  • Game advances in monthly increments
  • 12 months = 1 year
  • Game spans from 2025 to 2100 (75 years / 900 months maximum)

CO2 Calculation

Each month:

New CO2 = Current CO2 + Base Increase + Feedback Effects - Project Reductions
  • Base Increase: 0.3-0.7 ppm/month (depends on difficulty)
  • Feedback Effects: Extra CO2 from tipping points and climate feedbacks
  • Project Reductions: CO2 absorbed by your active projects
  • CO2 can never drop below 280 ppm (pre-industrial minimum)

Temperature Calculation

Temperature = (CO2 - 280) × 0.008°C

Examples:

CO2 Level Calculation Temperature
420 ppm (420 - 280) × 0.008 +1.12°C
450 ppm (450 - 280) × 0.008 +1.36°C
655 ppm (655 - 280) × 0.008 +3.0°C (LOSE)
405 ppm (405 - 280) × 0.008 +1.0°C (WIN)

To win, you must reduce CO2 to approximately 405 ppm or below.

5 Monthly Turn Sequence

When you click "Next Month", the following happens in order:

  1. 1 Time Advances: Month increments (year wraps at month 13)
  2. 2 CO2 Increases: Base increase + climate feedback effects added
  3. 3 Projects Process: Each region's projects calculate CO2 reductions and income
  4. 4 CO2 Reduced: Total project reductions subtracted from global CO2
  5. 5 Temperature Updates: Global and regional temperatures recalculated
  6. 6 Tipping Points Check: New tipping points triggered if thresholds crossed
  7. 7 Events Process: Active events tick down; new events may trigger
  8. 8 Research Points: Research Centers generate RP for technology unlocks
  9. 9 Campaigns Progress: Active campaigns advance; completed ones increase dedication
  10. 10 Income Added: Country contributions + project income (modified by events)
  11. 11 Achievements Check: New achievements unlocked if conditions met
  12. 12 Win/Lose Check: Game checks victory and defeat conditions
  13. 13 Auto-Save: Progress automatically saved to browser storage
6 Climate Tipping Points

What Are Tipping Points?

Tipping points are irreversible climate thresholds. Once triggered, they permanently accelerate warming by adding extra CO2 each month. These represent real-world climate feedback mechanisms that scientists warn about.

Tipping Point Thresholds

Threshold Name Effect
+1.5°C Arctic Sea Ice Decline +0.05 ppm/month (reduced reflectivity)
+2.0°C Permafrost Methane Release +0.1 ppm/month (ancient methane)
+2.5°C Amazon Rainforest Dieback +0.08 ppm/month, forest projects 50% less effective
+2.8°C Ocean Circulation Weakening +0.12 ppm/month (weather disruption)

Warning: The tipping points panel shows warnings when you're within 0.3°C of a threshold!

Climate Feedback Loops

In addition to tipping points, gradual feedback loops add extra warming as conditions worsen:

  • Ice-Albedo Feedback: Starts at +1.5°C - melting ice reduces Earth's reflectivity
  • Ocean Saturation: Starts at 450 ppm - warmer oceans absorb less CO2
  • Vegetation Stress: Starts at +2.0°C - heat-stressed plants absorb less carbon

Regional Effectiveness

Each region has potential scores (0-1) for different project types:

Cost Multiplier:

Adjusted Cost = Base Cost × (2 - Potential Score)

Effect Multiplier:

Adjusted Effect = Base Effect × Potential Score

Key insight: Higher potential = lower cost AND higher effectiveness (double benefit!)

10 Natural Disasters

Temperature-Driven Disasters

Natural disasters occur in regions around the world, with probability scaling based on global temperature. Higher temperatures mean more frequent and severe disasters.

Disaster Probability:

Probability = Base Rate × Vulnerability × Temp Multiplier

Disaster Types

Disaster Vulnerable Regions Income Impact Duration
Heat Wave Middle East, South Asia, Mediterranean, Australia -15% 2-3 months
Hurricane/Cyclone Caribbean, Gulf Coast, Southeast Asia, Pacific Islands -25% 1-2 months

Extreme Cold Northern Europe, Russia, Northern US/Canada -10% 1-2 months

Climate Awareness Effect

Regions that experience disasters become more aware of climate change, making Climate Policy Campaigns easier in those regions:

Disaster Severity Awareness Boost Effect
Minor (10-15% income loss) +3% Campaigns 3% easier
Moderate (20% income loss) +5% Campaigns 5% easier
Severe (25%+ income loss) +8% Campaigns 8% easier

Strategic tip: Focus campaigns on disaster-affected regions for better results. Regions without disasters maintain their base campaign difficulty.

Infrastructure Resilience

Wealthier countries with better infrastructure suffer less damage from disasters. Disaster impact is modified by each region's resilience score.

Note: Disasters are disabled in Tutorial mode and reduced in Easy mode.

11 Climate Finance System

Real-World Climate Finance

Income is based on each country's climate dedication - the percentage of their GDP they commit to climate action.

Monthly Income = (GDP × Climate %) ÷ 12

Climate Policy Campaigns

Increase a country's climate dedication through political advocacy:

Campaign Cost:

Cost = GDP × 0.5% × Difficulty

Campaign Duration:

Duration = 12 months × Difficulty × Political Resistance

Easier countries (Germany, France, Sweden) are cheaper and faster to influence than difficult ones (Saudi Arabia, Russia).

16 Strategy Tips

Key Strategic Insights

  • Avoid tipping points at all costs: They're irreversible and compound the problem.
  • Build Research Centers early: Technology upgrades provide permanent benefits.
  • Focus on high-potential regions: Solar in Saudi Arabia gives 3x+ more value.
  • Boost income with campaigns: Run campaigns in large, easy countries first.
  • Balance income and reduction: Need steady income to afford powerful projects.
  • Target 405 ppm CO2: That's the threshold for +1.0°C and victory.
  • Watch for breakthrough events: They can dramatically improve your projects.

Recommended Build Order

  1. Build 1-2 Research Centers for RP generation
  2. Run campaigns in large GDP countries (USA, China, Germany)
  3. Build Solar/Wind in high-potential regions for income
  4. Unlock Advanced Photovoltaics (100 RP) for solar bonus
  5. Scale up Carbon Capture in industrial regions
  6. Rush Direct Air Capture (1000 RP) for 2x carbon removal

Math to Win (Normal Mode)

Starting at 420 ppm, you need to reach 405 ppm (a reduction of 15 ppm).

With Direct Air Capture tech (2x), each Carbon Capture facility removes 8 ppm/month!

  • 2 Carbon Capture facilities: -16 ppm/month
  • Minus base increase: -16 + 0.5 = -15.5 ppm/month
  • Time to win: ~1 month once built!

The challenge is surviving long enough to afford and research the technology!
